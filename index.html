About

This is a small network operated by Guy. I'm a DevOps engineer and IPv6 enthusiast based in High Wycombe, United Kingdom. Most network traffic will come from my homelab, and a few VLANs of my home network setup.

Why

Why manage my own network? Mainly because I find it fun. As a person working in Platform/Devops engineering, I get to play with a lot of technologies as part of my work. However, networking usually isn't one of them. So I do it as a hobby in my free time.

Additionally, IPv6 implementation in the UK is very much lacking, with many ISPs relying on CG-NAT to extend the use of IPv4 since the global exhaustion of IPv4 addresses.

Also, I want to give my family and friends alternatives to "cloud" platforms. I like data independence/sovereignty, and think people should be more empowered to manage their own data.

Contact

In case things went horribly wrong, and my networking config is whacky, please send an email directly to noc@as199109.net. This address is monitored at least daily.

Peering & Routing Policy

I currently have a presence in the following IXPs:

  • InterIX - Amsterdam
  • BGP.Exchange - London
  • BGP.Exchange - Frankfurt
  • 4IXP - Zurich

If you wish to peer at any of these exchanges, please email peering@as199109.net. I originate only 2001:678:c64::/48 from AS199109, and maintain RPKI and ASPA to validate what other ASes can announce me. All routes are subject to strict RPKI filtering, only allowing RPKI valid routes.

I also have the following transit providers:

  • Olilo (AS212683)
  • Andrews and Arnold (AS20712)
  • Hurricane Electric (AS6939)
  • ParadoxNetworks (AS52025)
  • Route64 (AS212895)
  • HyeHost (AS47272)
  • FreeTransit Project (AS41051)
